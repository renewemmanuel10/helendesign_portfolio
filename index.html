The Atelier & Vision

Meet Master Stylist Simone Laurent

With more than a decade curating runway looks for Paris, London, and New York Fashion Weeks, Simone established Élégance on a single uncompromising thesis: extraordinary hair architecture must never sacrifice follicular health.

As a certified clinical trichologist and artisan braider, Simone engineers each parting with scientific weight-distribution and uses cold-pressed botanical infusions to nurture root density and scalp vitality.
